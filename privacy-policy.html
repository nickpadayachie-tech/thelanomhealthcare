1. Introduction and scope

Thelanom Healthcare Services ("Thelanom", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable South African law.

This policy applies to personal information collected through this website, appointment enquiries, telephone calls, WhatsApp messages and other communications with the clinic. It should be read together with our Cookie Policy and Medical Disclaimer.

Clinical records created when you attend the clinic are handled within the clinic's professional healthcare environment and are subject to patient confidentiality and applicable health-record requirements. This website is not intended to be a channel for medical records.

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact and identity information: your full name, telephone number and email address.
  • Enquiry details: your preferred service, preferred consulting location, preferred appointment date and any general message you choose to include.
  • Communication information: information you provide when you call, email or message the clinic by WhatsApp.
  • Website preferences: your cookie consent choices and related preference records.
  • Technical information: information such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited, but only where analytics is enabled and you have consented. The hosting provider may also process standard server logs [hosting arrangements to be confirmed].
  • Clinical information: health information you provide when attending the clinic or communicating with healthcare staff in a clinical context. This is special personal information and is handled separately from website enquiries.

Please do not send sensitive medical information through the website enquiry form, email or WhatsApp. The general enquiry form asks only for the information needed to respond to your enquiry and arrange an appointment.

Where an appointment concerns a baby or child, the appointment should be arranged by a parent or legal guardian. Please do not submit a child's health information through the website form.

4. Purpose of processing

We process personal information for the following purposes, as applicable:

  • To respond to appointments, enquiries and requests for information.
  • To arrange, coordinate and provide healthcare services in a clinical setting.
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or care.
  • To maintain and improve the quality and security of our website and services.
  • To manage complaints, feedback and legal or regulatory requirements.
  • To comply with applicable law and professional obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use website enquiry information for unrelated marketing.

5. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal information only where a lawful basis applies under POPIA, including:

  • Consent: where you have agreed to processing, for example optional analytics cookies or certain communications.
  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: where processing is necessary to respond to your request or provide healthcare services you have requested.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required by law to process or retain information.
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for the proper management of the clinic and does not unfairly affect your rights.
  • Vital interests: where processing is necessary to protect a person's life or health in an emergency.
  • Healthcare purposes: where health information is processed by healthcare professionals for medical purposes, subject to POPIA and professional confidentiality obligations.

Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it at any time, subject to law and any contractual or legal obligations.

6. Special personal information and health information

Health information is treated as special personal information under POPIA. We process it only where lawful and necessary, with appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards, and generally in connection with your clinical care.

The website enquiry form is not a medical record and is not designed to receive health information. You should not include detailed symptoms, diagnoses, medication lists or other sensitive health information in the form, by email or by WhatsApp unless the clinic has asked you to do so and has agreed an appropriate method.

If you have a medical concern, please contact the clinic to arrange an appropriate consultation or seek urgent medical attention where necessary.

7. Patient confidentiality

Patient confidentiality is fundamental to healthcare. Access to patient information is restricted to people who need it for legitimate clinical, administrative or legal purposes. Healthcare professionals and staff are expected to maintain confidentiality in accordance with applicable professional and legal obligations.

Website enquiries are handled separately from clinical records. We do not store patient records in browser local storage, and we do not use the website as a clinical record system.

8. Sharing of personal information

We may share personal information only where necessary and lawful, including with:

  • Healthcare professionals involved in your care, where appropriate and with your knowledge or as permitted by law.
  • Service providers that support our website, hosting, communications or administration, subject to appropriate confidentiality and processing terms [service providers to be confirmed].
  • Regulators, courts, law enforcement or other public authorities where required or permitted by law.
  • Emergency services or healthcare providers where necessary to protect a person's life or health.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

9. Third-party services and external links

This website uses links to third-party services, including Google Business Profile, Google Maps and WhatsApp. If you click an external link, the third party's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control those services.

  • Google: used for the Google Business Profile, Google Maps directions and, only with your consent and where configured, Google Analytics 4.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): used when you choose to contact the clinic by WhatsApp.
  • Website hosting: our hosting provider processes website data on our behalf [hosting provider to be confirmed].

Some third-party providers may process personal information outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we require an appropriate level of protection as required by POPIA. Specific cross-border arrangements must be confirmed by the clinic.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. These measures include access controls, secure website transmission where HTTPS is enabled, and restrictions on who may access information.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information through unsecured channels. If you become aware of a potential security concern relating to this website, please contact the clinic immediately.

If a security compromise affecting personal information occurs, we will assess and respond in accordance with applicable law, including POPIA's notification requirements where they apply.

11. Retention and deletion

12. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To exercise a right, contact the clinic using the details in section 2. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions, including healthcare confidentiality and record-retention obligations.
