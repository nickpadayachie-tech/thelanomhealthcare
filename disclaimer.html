Medical Disclaimer

Please read this disclaimer before relying on any information on this website or sending a general enquiry.

Version 1.0 Last updated: 18 November 2026

If you have an emergency or urgent medical concern, do not use this website. Call 10177 (ambulance) or 112 from a mobile phone, or go to your nearest emergency department.

1. General information only

The content on this website is provided for general information about Thelanom Healthcare Services and the services offered by the clinic. It is not intended to be complete, individualised medical advice.

2. Not a substitute for professional medical advice

Website content does not replace consultation, assessment, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional. You should not delay seeking professional medical advice because of something you have read on this website.

3. Individual treatment requires assessment

Individual treatment decisions require a professional assessment of your specific circumstances. Information on this website cannot determine whether a particular service, treatment or approach is appropriate for you. Please contact the clinic to arrange an appropriate consultation where needed.

4. Website enquiries are not emergency medical services

The website, its contact links and the appointment enquiry form are not emergency medical services and are not monitored continuously. Do not send urgent or emergency information through the website form, email or WhatsApp and wait for a response.

For urgent medical concerns:

  • Call 10177 for an ambulance.
  • Call 112 from any mobile phone.
  • Go to the nearest emergency department or healthcare facility.

5. Appointment enquiries

Submitting an appointment enquiry does not confirm an appointment, create a clinical relationship or guarantee availability. The clinic must contact you to confirm the appointment and any relevant details.

Please do not include detailed medical or health information in the general enquiry form. It is not a secure clinical communication channel.

6. Accuracy and completeness

We aim to keep the information on this website accurate and up to date, but information may change and may not always be complete. Service descriptions, operating arrangements and contact details should be confirmed directly with the clinic.

This website links to third-party services such as Google Business Profile, Google Maps and WhatsApp. These services are not controlled by Thelanom Healthcare Services, and their own terms and privacy policies apply. We do not endorse or take responsibility for third-party content.

8. Professional information

Professional details and training listed on this website are provided by the clinic. Please contact the clinic to confirm current professional registration, qualifications and practice details.

9. Limitation and your rights

To the extent permitted by law, Thelanom Healthcare Services does not accept liability for loss or harm arising from reliance on general information on this website or from the use of external links. Nothing in this disclaimer limits any rights you have under applicable law or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

This disclaimer may be updated from time to time. The current version and update date are shown at the top of this page. For questions about this disclaimer, contact the clinic on 083 435 0021 or through the contact page.
