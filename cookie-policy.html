This Cookie Policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, such as browser local storage. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, can store small amounts of information in your browser. They can help a website function, remember preferences or understand how visitors use the site.

This website does not use cookies or similar technologies to store patient records or sensitive medical information.

2. Your choices

When you first visit the website, you can choose to:

  • Accept all: allow optional analytics and marketing cookies.
  • Reject non-essential: allow only essential storage.
  • Manage preferences: choose each optional category individually.

Optional categories are not preselected. No analytics or marketing scripts are loaded before you give consent, and choosing "Reject non-essential" is presented as clearly as accepting all cookies.

Your choice is stored in your browser using local storage under the key thelanom_cookie_consent, together with the policy version and the date of your choice.

3. Essential storage

Essential storage is required for the website to remember your cookie choices and to support basic security. It does not track you for advertising or analytics.

Essential storage used by this website
Category Purpose Example Status
Essential Remembers your cookie consent choice and policy version. Local storage key thelanom_cookie_consent Always active

The essential consent record remains in your browser until you clear it, change your preferences or a new policy version requires a fresh choice.

4. Analytics

Analytics helps the clinic understand how the website is used so that it can be improved. Analytics cookies are optional and are only activated if a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is configured and you have given consent.

Analytics cookies
Category Purpose Examples Status
Analytics Measures website visits and usage to help improve the site. _ga, _ga_<container-id> (Google Analytics 4) Off by default; used only with consent

Analytics is not currently active unless a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is configured and you consent. The exact cookie durations depend on the analytics configuration [to be confirmed]. Google may process analytics data outside South Africa, subject to its own safeguards and terms.

5. Marketing

Marketing cookies may be used to measure or personalise advertising. No marketing cookies are currently configured on this website. If marketing cookies are introduced, they will remain off unless you consent, and this policy will be updated.

Marketing cookies
Category Purpose Examples Status
Marketing Measures or personalises advertising where used. None currently configured Off by default

6. Third-party services

External links to Google Business Profile, Google Maps and WhatsApp are not cookies. If you choose to open those links, the relevant third party's privacy policy and terms apply.

Where analytics is enabled and consented to, Google Analytics 4 is provided by Google. The website may also be supported by a hosting provider that processes standard technical information on the clinic's behalf [hosting provider to be confirmed].

7. Manage or withdraw your preferences

You can change or withdraw your cookie choices at any time by opening the cookie settings:

The same option is available through the Cookie Settings link in the website footer. If you withdraw consent for analytics, the clinic will stop using analytics cookies and will take steps to remove existing analytics cookies where technically possible.

You can also delete or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect how the cookie banner and preference controls work.

8. Duration and updates

Your consent record is stored with policy version 1.0. If this policy changes materially or a new consent version is introduced, you may be asked to make a fresh choice. You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove the stored consent record.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The current version and update date are shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy or your privacy choices, contact Thelanom Healthcare Services on 083 435 0021 or by WhatsApp. The business email address and Information Officer details are marked [to be confirmed] in the Privacy Policy.
