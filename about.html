About Thelanom

Care grounded in compassion and respect.

Thelanom Healthcare Services is a private nursing and healthcare clinic serving women, mothers, babies and families in Sebokeng and Vanderbijlpark.

Our Story

Healthcare that begins with listening.

We understand that healthcare appointments can feel personal, and sometimes uncertain. Our approach is to create a calm, respectful space where you can ask questions, share your concerns and feel supported in the decisions that affect your health.

From pregnancy and postnatal care to baby wellness, family planning and general nursing services, Thelanom Healthcare Services offers professional care across two convenient consulting locations. Every patient is treated as an individual, with dignity and attention to their specific needs.

Our commitment is simple: compassionate care, clear communication and a patient-centred approach at every visit.

Our Approach

What patient-centred care means to us.

These principles guide the way we welcome, listen to and support every patient who visits Thelanom Healthcare Services.

Dignity and respect

Every person is treated with warmth, privacy and respect, regardless of their reason for visiting the clinic.

Clear communication

We take time to listen carefully and explain information in a way that is clear, calm and easy to understand.

Support at every step

From your first enquiry to your follow-up care, we aim to make your experience as comfortable and supported as possible.

Professional Profile

Professional nursing care, delivered personally.

Thelanom Healthcare Services is led by Sr N.P. Matena, a registered nursing professional with practice number 0034169. The clinic’s professional credentials include registered nursing qualifications in General, Psychiatric, Community and Midwifery nursing.

At Thelanom Healthcare Services, we believe healthcare should be personal, compassionate and centred on the individual. We strive to provide a welcoming, confidential and respectful environment where every patient feels comfortable discussing their concerns, asking questions and exploring their healthcare options.

Our approach is guided by attentive care, professional understanding and a commitment to helping patients make informed decisions about their health and well-being.

For enquiries regarding professional registration, practice information or available healthcare services, please contact Thelanom Healthcare Services directly.
