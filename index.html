Welcome to Thelanom

Healthcare that begins with listening.

Thelanom Healthcare Services is a private nursing and healthcare clinic serving individuals and families in Sebokeng and Vanderbijlpark. Our approach is built around respectful, patient-centred care, taking time to understand your needs and offering clear guidance at every step.

From pregnancy and postnatal support to baby wellness, family planning and general nursing care, we aim to create a calm and caring environment where you feel heard and supported.

  • Compassionate care for women, mothers, babies and families
  • Professional nursing services in two convenient locations
  • A patient-centred approach that respects your individual needs
