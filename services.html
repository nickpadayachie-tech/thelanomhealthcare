Our Services

Professional care for women, mothers, babies and families.

Thelanom Healthcare Services offers nine approved healthcare services at our consulting locations in Sebokeng and Vanderbijlpark.

Healthcare Services

Support for every stage of life.

Each service is provided with privacy, professionalism and a patient-centred approach. Contact the clinic if you are unsure which service is right for you.

Antenatal Care and Childbirth Education

Pregnancy care, antenatal support and childbirth education. Antenatal visits offer an opportunity to discuss your pregnancy, ask questions and prepare for childbirth with professional guidance.

Enquire about antenatal care

Postnatal Care

Healthcare and support for mothers and babies following childbirth, with attention to the health and wellbeing of both mother and baby.

Enquire about postnatal care

Well-Baby Clinic and Immunisation

Baby wellness services and immunisation to support your child's health and development, with guidance for parents and caregivers.

Enquire about the well-baby clinic

Family Planning

Contraception and reproductive health guidance, provided confidentially and with respect for your individual choices and circumstances.

Enquire about family planning

HIV Management

Confidential HIV testing, counselling and patient support in a private setting, with clear information and respectful guidance.

Enquire about HIV Management

Wound Care

Wound assessment, treatment and appropriate ongoing care to support healing, with clear guidance on what to expect.

Enquire about wound care

Minor Ailments

Assessment and advice for common health complaints, with referral where necessary. If your symptoms are urgent or severe, seek appropriate emergency medical attention.

Enquire about minor ailments

Pregnancy Testing

Pregnancy testing and related healthcare guidance in a private and supportive setting, with an opportunity to discuss your next steps.

Enquire about pregnancy testing

Pap Smear

Cervical screening services provided with privacy, sensitivity and professional care, with clear information about the process.

Enquire about a Pap smear
